## [^55] Buy Airplane Tickets When Everyone Is Sleeping to Save Money
**Verdict:** SOFTEN
**Reason:** The "book on Tuesday/Wednesday" and "book at 1am" airfare tips are a widely repeated myth that later data (Hopper, Google Flights, Expedia) has debunked — day-of-week pricing advantages are marginal-to-nonexistent on most routes, and how far in advance you book matters more than which day or hour you book.
