**Verdict:** SOFTEN
**Reason:** This 2014 Consumer Reports chart is compared against "FDA and EPA guidelines" of the time, but FDA/EPA fish advice has since been substantively revised (a 2021/2024 update replaced the older approach with a 62-fish "Best Choices / Good Choices / Fish to Avoid" chart and different serving recommendations for pregnant/nursing women — see fda.gov/food/consumers/advice-about-eating-fish), so the specific "eat even less than FDA/EPA recommend" comparison is now dated even though the general low-mercury fish list (shellfish, wild salmon, tilapia) still tracks current guidance reasonably well.
