**Verdict:** SOFTEN
**Reason:** "promises to keep the body active and growing stronger" is overconfident/guarantee-style language for a generic workout plan; the underlying advice (a trainer-informed 20-minute daily routine) is still reasonable and timeless, so soften the wording rather than archive. (Note: the live Lifehacker page also carries an editor's note that an interactive tool embedded in the original 2012 post is broken, but the field guide text doesn't reference that tool directly, so no archive is needed on that basis.)
**Find this exact text in the doc:**
"""
Getting into an exercise habit doesn't have to mean an hour at the gym: a daily 20-minute plan built with input from trainers and fitness experts promises to keep the body active and growing stronger without heavy equipment, varying the routine day to day to stave off boredom[^119].
"""
**Replace with:**
"""
Getting into an exercise habit doesn't have to mean an hour at the gym: a daily 20-minute plan built with input from trainers and fitness experts is designed to keep the body active and growing stronger without heavy equipment, varying the routine day to day to stave off boredom[^119].
"""
